  on the civic path. The ranges below are order-of-magnitude scenarios from
  voting_cost_estimate.md. This repo does not ship a measured submitBatch snapshot
  in that document. Pass --gas-report for Foundry gas on the key calls.

  L1TallyMirror.postResult                       40,000–100,000       $0.0137      $0.0343
