Election clerk

clerk

Create an election, then freeze the roll before polls open. maxParticipants = 0 means unlimited. Choose plurality (default) or ranked choice (IRV).

If this Node has CHAIN_RPC set, Create election also calls ElectionFactory.createElection and stores the on-chain uint256 id next to the UUID. Factory failures are shown here and nothing is saved locally. Without CHAIN_RPC this form writes zk-voting.json only (demo) and still mints a demo onChainElectionId for the circuit.

Person identity

one person · one active ETH

A person is unique. Attest a passkey, Web3 wallet, or smart-card digital ID. Many Ethereum addresses may be internally verified; only one is active. The election Merkle leaf remains an identity commitment — never the active address, never a legal name. Person keys are keccak256 of a civil-file token; this form does not store a name.

Tally operator

tally_operator

Post per-question plurality counts after cutoff. Each question is tallied on its own options — do not sum every contest into one vector that must equal accepted ballots. Ranked-choice questions are computed with IRV from accepted ranking envelopes (leave the plurality vector blank on an all-ranked ballot). On opens_ballots elections, guardians enroll, publish Feldman deals, and after cutoff submit partial decryptions. t of n must verify before the Node opens envelopes. Invented partitions are rejected. Homomorphic vs mixnet aggregation is not decided. NON-PRODUCTION. If L1_RPC, L1_TALLY_MIRROR_ADDRESS, and L1_PUBLISHER_PRIVATE_KEY are set, the Node also copies keccak256(abi.encodePacked(counts)) to L1TallyMirror. Unset L1 env is a no-op.

Choose an election to see the guardian ceremony.
Concatenated plurality options in ballot order, or use the per-question fields above. For an all-ranked ballot, leave blank to compute IRV from envelopes. On opens_ballots, omit to derive counts from opened envelopes; invented partitions are rejected. Example YES_NO → [yesCount, noCount].
